## 🏛️ Executive Summary & Master Thesis

> **Taking AI to the next level is not about prompting chatbots; it is about building bounded, auditable IT workflows where AI retrieves, analyzes, and prepares work under strict human governance—while keeping the Operational Technology (OT) air gap absolute.**

```text
      [ OPERATIONAL TECHNOLOGY (OT) ]                [ ENTERPRISE IT NETWORK ]
       SCADA • Substations • Breakers               CIS/Billing • Ticketing • Docs
                      │                                            │
   ═══════════════════╪════════════════════════════════════════════╪══════════════════
                      │  STRICT AIR-GAP                            │
               NO AI READ/WRITE                     AI OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY
                                                    • Bounded Agentic Pipelines
                                                    • Local Inference (Ollama/vLLM)
                                                    • Human-in-the-Loop Approval
```
